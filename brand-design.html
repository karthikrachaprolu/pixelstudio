BRAND DESIGN

More than a logo. A complete identity.

A strong brand is more than a logo or a color. It is the visual language that communicates who you are, what you represent and why people should remember you.


PixelStudio develops cohesive brand systems that can work across websites, social media, marketing materials and digital experiences.

Colour System

A structured set of colors that defines how a brand uses color consistently across its visual identity.

  • Primary Colors — main brand colors
  • Secondary Colors — supporting colors
  • Accent Colors — highlights and calls-to-action
  • Neutral Colors — black, white, gray and backgrounds
  • Light / Dark Variations
  • Colour Codes — HEX, RGB, CMYK and HSL
  • Usage Rules — where and how each color should be used
